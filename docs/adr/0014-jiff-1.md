# ADR-0014: jiff 1.x, and `nepdate` without its `jiff` feature for now

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Approved by:** Sanam
- **Amends:** ADR-0010 point 3 (`nepdate` is used without the `jiff` feature until a release
  builds it on jiff 1.x) and ADR-0004 (the `app` functions take a `jiff::ZonedDateTime`, the jiff
  1.0 name for `jiff::Zoned`)

## Context

MitiBar reads the clock and the time zones through jiff. jiff 1.0 is out and promises to stay on
1.x. The breaking change that reaches MitiBar is the rename of `Zoned` to `ZonedDateTime`.

MitiBar also used the `jiff` feature of the published `nepdate` crate to turn a jiff date into a
BS date and back. The `nepdate` release MitiBar pins builds that feature on jiff 0.2. jiff 0.2 and
jiff 1 are different crates to Cargo, so a jiff 1 date does not match those conversions, and
keeping the feature would also build both jiff majors into the app.

## Decision

1. MitiBar depends on `jiff = "1"`, with the default features as before.
2. MitiBar depends on `nepdate` without the `jiff` feature. `app::bs_date` converts between
   `jiff::civil::Date` and `NepaliDate` through the AD year, month and day, the same way the
   feature does.
3. MitiBar keeps depending on published crates only (ADR-0010). When a `nepdate` release builds
   its `jiff` feature on jiff 1.x, MitiBar may turn the feature back on and drop `app::bs_date`.

## Consequences

- One jiff, 1.x, in the app.
- Two small conversion functions live in the app until the next `nepdate` release; their tests
  cover the edges of the supported range.
- The MSRV stays Rust 1.92 (Slint). jiff 1.x needs Rust 1.85.
